DATA DICTIONARY

S1 scale_effects_49_contrasts.csv
method: intervention and its comparison baseline.
model: evaluated generator and nominal size.
metric: spider_ts, spider_ex, spider_em, bird_ex, bird_ex_no_ek, bird_ex_ek,
  bird_fs_ex (correct-schema diagnostic), cosql_dev_qm or cosql_dev_im.
  CoSQL QM measures questions; IM measures complete interactions. They are not EX.
baseline/result: reported percentage endpoints under the documented condition.
gain_pp: result minus baseline in percentage points, not relative percent change.
main_table: True for the 16 main Table 4 contrasts; False for supplementary ones.

S2 compact_configurations_24.csv
system_generator: exact generator/training/retrieval condition in Table 9.
BIRD_dev_EX: reported development execution accuracy; paired values retain
  no-EK / with-EK order. NR means the exact condition was not established.
Spider_dev_EX: reported Spider development execution accuracy; NR is retained
  for TS-only, test-only or otherwise unavailable development EX.
inference_search_auxiliaries: beams, SQL samples, rollouts, revision limits and
  online helper modules. NR and adaptive counts are intentionally preserved.
numerical_source_venue: citation number, source table and version/venue note.
Some cells preserve LaTeX-style scholarly notation from the manuscript.

S3 source_manifest.json
file/url: pinned numerical source artifact and public retrieval URL.
bytes/pages/sha256: exact retrieved PDF identity; these are provenance records,
  not embedded third-party files or claims of reproduction.

S4/S5
Raw author-reported endpoints and conditional extraction notes. Stable source
tags in S4 are distinct from renumbered journal reference IDs; use S6 to trace
earlier working reference numbers. Missingness must not be imputed.

S6
old_to_new: earlier working citation number to final first-appearance number.
current_references: bibliographic entries and original working IDs, not new
numerical evaluations.
