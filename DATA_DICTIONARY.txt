DATA DICTIONARY - 15 October 2026

S1 scale_effects.csv
method/model: the source-reported intervention, comparison and generator.
metric: named dataset/split and source metric. spider_ts is test-suite accuracy;
spider_em and robustness *_em are exact match; *_ex is execution. *_qem/iem
are question/interaction match; *_qex/iex are question/interaction execution.
baseline/result: reported percentage endpoints; gain_pp: result minus baseline.
main_table: True for 22 main Table 4 contrasts, False for other contrasts.
source_reference: CURRENT manuscript reference number, not the stable S4 tag.
source_location/conditions: primary table/page, input/search and comparison
scope. Legacy source tags and original extraction details remain in S4.

S2 compact_configurations.csv
system_generator: exact Table 9 training/retrieval/sampling configuration.
BIRD_dev_EX: development execution accuracy; CodeS pairs retain no-EK / with-EK.
Spider_dev_EX: development execution accuracy, never TS, dialogue or test EX.
inference_search_auxiliaries: beam, samples, revision/search and online helpers.
numerical_source_venue: CURRENT manuscript citation, table and source version.
NR means the exact value/condition was not established. No imputation.

S3 source_manifest.json: URL/file/bytes/pages/SHA256 identify the retrieved primary
PDF artifact. These records are provenance, not redistribution of those PDFs.

S4/S5: raw source-reported endpoints, conditions, scope and unresolved source
issues. The legacy 16-condition S4 subset is explicitly historical; use S2 for
the current 37-condition inventory. Offline synthesis and student training costs
are different quantities. Figure trends without exact labels are not digitized.

S6 current_references: current first-appearance reference number to bibliography.
old_to_new: original working IDs that still refer to retained source identities.
previous_release_to_current: immediately previous manuscript number to current.
retired_references: source removed when size attribution was corrected. Newly
added papers do not inherit the identity of the removed secondary citation.
